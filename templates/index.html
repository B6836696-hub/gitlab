สูตรที่ใช้ (Thevenin base divider)

VBB = VCC · R2 / (R1 + R2)
RB  = R1 ∥ R2
IB  = (VBB − VBE) / (RB + (β + 1)·RE)
IC  = β·IB
IE  = (β + 1)·IB
VE  = IE·RE
VB  = VE + VBE
VC  = VCC − IC·RC
VCE = VC − VE
